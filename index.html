Article I — The Founding Decree

“Let it be known across every province: the crown of Kharnholt answers to no council, no committee, no fractured parliament. One throne. One iron hand. One reign that does not waver, does not bargain, does not kneel.”

— Proclaimed from the Obsidian Hall

Article II — The Three Pillars

What the Reign Is Built On

Absolute Order

No committee delays the crown. Decisions are made once, by one authority, and carried out without debate.

Forged Strength

Every province is rebuilt under the same iron discipline — infrastructure, defense, and will, unified under a single doctrine.

Undying Will

Thrones fall when rulers waver. This one does not negotiate with doubt, and will not be the exception that breaks.
