Est. 2025
01 — Intro

Background in graphic design, UX experience before this, now living at the intersection of design, motion and AI tooling — building the templates and pipelines other designers use, not just one-off visuals.

I design and ship multilingual flyers, social content, and motion assets for one of Canada's major furniture retailers, and build the custom tooling — InDesign scripting, browser-based template engines, AI image pipelines — that make that production faster for the whole team. Outside of that, I take on client-facing creative work and the occasional award-submission concept piece.
